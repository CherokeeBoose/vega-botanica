Ritual · The scent

It opens the way a garden smells in the minute after the heat leaves it. Cool, green, still deciding.

Then the jasmine arrives, and it is not the soap-and-linen kind. It is the real kind: heavy, honeyed, heady, the note that made perfumers say jasmine is the one flower that smells human.

It stays there a long while. The florals beneath it give it weight and keep it from going airless. Underneath, a warm wood holds the whole thing against the skin.

By the fourth hour it has stopped behaving like a fragrance and started behaving like a temperature.

Sun-kissed white florals, married with warm wood. Like honeysuckle on a summer fence. Sweet, and not easily forgotten.

Why the batches are small

Jasmine will not be hurried, and it will not be scaled.

The flower opens after sunset and is picked in the dark, by hand, because by sunrise most of what you wanted from it has already left. Everything about the material argues for small quantities and short runs.

Ritual is made the same way. One batch at a time, filled by hand, and when it is gone it is gone until the next pour.

3 A.M.

When picking begins. The flowers are gathered before first light and taken in within hours.

1000 KG

Roughly the weight of fresh blossom behind a single kilogram of jasmine absolute.

300 +

Individual aromatic compounds in that one material. No synthetic reproduces all of them.

The founder

I have taught for most of my working life. More recently, yoga.

I meditate in my garden most mornings. That is where I learned the value of stillness — and yoga is what taught me how to turn that stillness into action. Ritual began there, somewhere between the two.

I am also an aerialist, which is how I know Sarah, who owns Flying Buddha Studio. Batch 001 exists because she told me to stop waiting for perfect and bring it to her Higher Vibes market instead. She was right.

I formulate, mix, rest, filter, bottle and number every batch myself. That sets a natural ceiling on how much there can be, and that is the point. I would rather run out than pour something I would not wear.

Ritual is my first release. It took a long time to get the jasmine to sit exactly where I wanted it, and I am not in a hurry to add a second scent before this one is right.

Cher · Founder
